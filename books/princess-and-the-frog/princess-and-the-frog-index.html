Hop into the magical world of The Princess and the Frog with this delightful free coloring book! All 16 pages are filled with charming scenes from this beloved fairy tale — from moonlit lily pads and glittering swamps, to grand ballrooms, a crown-wearing frog, and a princess with a dream in her heart.

Whether you're a little one who loves frogs, a parent sharing a favourite story, or simply someone who enjoys a peaceful colouring session, these pages are ready and waiting. Pick up your pencils, crayons, or markers — and bring this classic tale to life in your own colours!
