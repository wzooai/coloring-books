About This Book

Step into the shadowy, golden world of Rumpelstiltskin with this wonderful free coloring book! All 17 pages bring to life the twists and turns of this classic fairy tale — from a miller's daughter locked in a tower room piled high with straw, to a mysterious little man spinning gold by candlelight, a grand royal hall, and a very satisfying ending where the right name is finally guessed!

Whether you're a little one captivated by the mystery, a parent who loves a tale full of riddles, or simply someone who enjoys bringing a storybook world to life with colour — these pages are ready for you. Grab your pencils or crayons and start spinning your own golden masterpiece!

🖨️ How to Print

  1. Download the free PDF using the button above.
  2. Open the file in any PDF viewer (Adobe Reader, Chrome, Preview).
  3. Print on standard letter or A4 paper — use the "Fit to Page" setting for best results.
  4. Color with pencils, markers, crayons, or watercolors — the thick outlines work with everything!
